About Our College

Karmaveer Adv. Baburao Ganpatrao Thakare College of Engineering (KBTCOE), Nashik, is a well-known engineering college. The college is affiliated with Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU), Pune and offers quality engineering education.

KBTCOE provides modern laboratories, a well-equipped library, experienced faculty, and good placement support. The college encourages students to develop technical skills, creativity, and leadership qualities.

Vision

To provide quality technical education and develop skilled, responsible, and innovative engineers for society.

Mission

  • Provide quality engineering education.
  • Develop technical and professional skills.
  • Encourage innovation and research.
  • Prepare students for successful careers.
